## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

`ezyHR` is an all-in-one Cloud HRMS and Singapore Payroll platform designed specifically for Singapore SMEs, growing enterprises, and multi-branch organizations. It streamlines the entire employee lifecycle—from onboarding and attendance tracking to leave management, claims, automated Singapore payroll (CPF, SDL, SHG, AIS/IR8A), and AI-driven HR insights.

## 4. Section Breakdown & Content Strategy

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline:** *"HR & Payroll, Simplified for Singapore."*
- **Supporting Tagline:** *"One intelligent platform to manage your people, payroll, attendance, leave, claims and compliance."*
- **Description:** *"ezyHR brings everyday HR operations and Singapore payroll into one secure cloud platform — helping businesses spend less time on administration and more time growing."*
- **CTAs:** Primary: `Book a Free Demo` (Opens Demo Modal), Secondary: `Start Free Trial`
- **Badges:** `Singapore Payroll` | `Cloud HRMS` | `AI-Powered` | `Mobile Ready`
- **Visual Asset:** Live interactive ezyHR HRMS Dashboard preview showing headcount, payroll status, attendance radar, and pending leave requests.

### Section 2: Trust Strip
- **Items:** Singapore-focused HRMS • Cloud Native • Automated Payroll Workflows • Role-Based Access Control • Mobile ESS App • Multi-Company Architecture
- **Rule:** Strict compliance — NO unverified statistics or fake customer numbers.

### Section 3: The Problem Statement
- **Heading:** *"HR shouldn't feel like spreadsheet management."*
- **Pain Points:** 8 interactive cards detailing manual payroll math, scattered attendance, manual leave tracking, lost employee docs, email claim chaos, missed compliance deadlines, spreadsheet overload, repetitive admin.
- **Transition:** *"ezyHR brings everything together into one unified cloud workspace."*

### Section 4: Platform Ecosystem
- **Heading:** *"One platform. One employee record. One source of truth."*
- **Modules (10):** Employee Management, Time & Attendance, Leave Management, Singapore Payroll, Claims & Expenses, Document Vault, Performance Tracking, Recruitment Pipeline, Workforce Analytics, AI HR Assistant.

### Section 5: Core HR
- **Heading:** *"Centralize your people data with confidence."*
- **Features:** Digital Employee Master Records, Employment History, Visual Org Charts, Employee Self-Service (ESS), Manager Self-Service (MSS), Document Storage & Automated Expiry Alerts, Custom Fields, Company Asset Tracking.

### Section 6: Time & Attendance
- **Heading:** *"Know who's working. Know what's happening."*
- **Features:** Mobile & Web Check-in, GPS Geo-fencing Attendance, Shift & Roster Scheduling, Overtime Calculation, Break Management, Automated Timesheets, Attendance Exception Reports.
- **Status Badges:** Clearly tags features as `AVAILABLE` or `COMING SOON`.

### Section 7: Leave Management
- **Heading:** *"Leave management without the paperwork."*
- **Features:** Real-time Leave Balances, One-click Approval Workflows, Visual Team Leave Calendar, Statutory Leave Tracking (Annual, Sick, Hospitalization, Childcare), Custom Leave Categories, Leave Analytics.

### Section 8 & 9: Singapore Payroll Engine & Interactive Simulator
- **Heading:** *"Singapore Payroll, without the spreadsheet headache."*
- **Workflow:** Employee Master → Attendance Sync → Leave & Claims → Automated Payroll Processing → Statutory Deductions (CPF, SDL, SHG) → IRAS AIS / IR8A Export → Bank GIRO File Generation → Itemized Mobile Payslips.
- **Compliance Wording:** *"Designed for Singapore payroll requirements"* and *"Supports Singapore statutory contribution rules"*.
- **Interactive Component:** Live Payroll Calculator Simulator with real-time gross/CPF/net pay calculation demo and sample payslip viewer labeled clearly with `DEMO DATA`.

### Section 10: AI HR Assistant
- **Heading:** *"Your HR assistant, always ready."*
- **Features:** Natural Language Policy Q&A, HR Insights Engine, Payroll Anomaly Detection, Attendance Pattern Insights, Smart Expiry Notifications, Document AI Search.
- **Badges:** Live status labels (`AVAILABLE` vs `COMING SOON`).

### Section 11: Mobile-First Employee Experience
- **Heading:** *"HR in your pocket. Built for modern teams."*
- **Mobile UI Views:** Clock-in/out, Submit Leave, View & Download Itemized Payslip, Submit Claims with Receipt Scan, Company Announcements, Manager Approvals.

### Section 12: Management Intelligence & Analytics
- **Heading:** *"Data-driven insights for leaders and HR teams."*
- **Metrics (DEMO DATA):** Live Headcount distribution, Attendance compliance, Leave utilization, Payroll cost analysis, Claims breakdown, Attrition trends.

### Section 13: Business Benefits & Impact
- **Focus:** 8 Core outcomes (Reduced admin burden, 100% payroll accuracy, Instant visibility, Centralized compliance, Modern employee experience).

### Section 14: Enterprise-Grade Security
- **Heading:** *"Security designed for modern businesses."*
- **Pillars:** Bank-grade Data Encryption (At Rest & In Transit), Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Comprehensive Audit Logs, Automated Cloud Backups, PDPA-aligned Privacy Framework.

### Section 15: Integrations
- **Heading:** *"Connect ezyHR with your existing software stack."*
- **Integrations:** Xero, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Slack, MS Teams, Biometric Attendance Terminals, REST APIs & Webhooks (Tagged `AVAILABLE` / `COMING SOON`).

### Section 16: Onboarding Roadmap
- **3 Steps:** `01 Configure` (Company structure & payroll rules) → `02 Import` (Seamless bulk employee & history import) → `03 Go Live` (Team onboarding & automated operations).

### Section 17: Pricing Architecture
- **Plans:** Starter (SMEs), Professional (Growing businesses), Enterprise (Multi-entity & Large Org).
- **CTA:** `Book a Free Demo` & `Contact Sales`. Pricing transparently marked as custom quote / contact sales.

### Section 18: FAQ Accordion
- 14 Comprehensive questions answering everything about cloud architecture, Singapore statutory payroll, CPF/IR8A compliance, mobile app access, security, multi-company support, and demo booking.

### Section 19 & 20: Final Conversion CTA & Footer

- **Footer:** Full compliance footer with parent company details (`REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD.`, UEN: `201723665M`), ezy SaaS trademark, contact info, privacy policy, terms of service.

## 7. Technical Implementation Stack & Guidelines

- **Framework:** React 18 + Vite + TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons (`lucide-react`)
- **Animation:** Clean CSS micro-interactions & Framer Motion
- **SEO & Metadata:** Helmet/head management, OpenGraph, JSON-LD Schema (Organization, SoftwareApplication, FAQPage)
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA (Keyboard navigable, focus rings, high contrast, ARIA labels, semantic markup)
- **Performance:** Optimized lazy loading, responsive images, bundle minimization (target >90 Lighthouse score).
