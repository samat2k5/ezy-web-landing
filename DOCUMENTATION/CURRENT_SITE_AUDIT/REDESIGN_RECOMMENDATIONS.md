# REDESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS REPORT

Based on our thorough audit of the current ezyHR web application, technology stack, statutory features, and branding assets, we recommend the following strategic framework for future landing page iterations:

---

## 1. Categorized Component Recommendations

### A. What Should Be RETAINED
- **Core Visual Identity & Color Palette:** Deep Navy (`#0F172A`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), and Royal Blue (`#2563EB`).
- **Brand Positioning & Legal Disclaimer:** ezyHR as a SaaS product operated under the **ezy SaaS** initiative of **REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD.** (Singapore UEN: **201723665M**).
- **Singapore Statutory Focus:** Deep emphasis on CPF, SDL, SHG, IRAS AIS/IR8A, Bank GIRO exports, and MOM itemized payslip compliance.
- **Interactive Singapore Payroll Calculator Simulator:** The live salary slider & CPF math simulator provides immense conversion value.
- **Content Safety Matrix Rules:** Zero unverified customer numbers, zero fake customer reviews, and clear `DEMO DATA` labeling.

### B. What Should Be REDESIGNED
- **Hero Section Visual Elevation:** Incorporate higher-density interactive dashboard mockups with live tab switching.
- **Mobile Employee Experience Preview:** Enhance mobile app frame mockups to show real-time GPS clock-in, leave balances, and expense receipt capture.
- **Interactive Module Ecosystem:** Expand the 10-module connected graph with dynamic feature previews.

### C. What Should Be IMPROVED
- **Form Analytics Integration:** Connect demo request form submissions to production CRM or webhook endpoints when ready.
- **Asset Bundle Code Splitting:** Utilize dynamic imports for heavy components to reduce initial bundle size below 500 kB.

### D. What Should Be REMOVED
- Any generic stock SaaS graphics or cartoon characters.
- Excessive glassmorphism or bright background gradients that distract from the enterprise aesthetic.

### E. What Should Be ADDED
- Additional localized case studies and customer testimonials once officially supplied by the product owner.
- Multi-currency SGD/MYR enterprise payroll preview options.
